The administrator's seat

Recognition, role clarity, and partnership for non-clinician GME professionals.

About this topic

Program administrators and GME professionals carry much of the operational weight in graduate medical education. The role keeps growing, and the recognition doesn't always keep pace.

Here I write about what the role is, where it's heading, and what real partnership between administrators and physician leaders looks like.
