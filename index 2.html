Why this exists

Most of what makes a training program work never shows up in the requirements. It lives in the program office: in the onboarding that went smoothly, the site visit that almost didn't, and the administrator who quietly held it all together.

I've spent about 20 years in GME administration. This is where I share what that work has taught me, for program administrators, GME leaders, and anyone who cares about the people who do this work.

What you'll find here

  • Stories from the field

    Real moments from GME, told with care and without identifying details.

  • The non-clinician perspective

    Where the program administrator role stands today and where it's heading.

  • Practical lessons

    Accreditation, oversight, and program operations, from someone who does the work.
